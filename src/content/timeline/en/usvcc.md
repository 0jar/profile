From January to May 2024 I worked as a Management Support Collaborator at the United States - Vietnam Cooperation Center. It was a short gig while I was still looking around, and mostly practical.

I helped with daily operations, dealt with visitors to the centre and handled the administration around events. It is the least technical entry here and probably the one where I learned most about how small organisations keep going. Someone has to greet the visitors, keep track of things and make sure it's in order.
