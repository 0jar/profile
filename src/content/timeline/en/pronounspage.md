Pronouns.page is a site about pronouns and inclusive language, and I have volunteered there since January 2023. I moderate content on the site and keep the Vietnamese version of it in shape.

The translation work is mostly research. A lot of the vocabulary has no settled Vietnamese equivalent, so part of the work is finding what people use, or if it doesn't exist in Vietnamese, looking at other languages (mainly English and Chinese) for terms to borrow. Then I keep everything consistent across the interface and the documentation.
