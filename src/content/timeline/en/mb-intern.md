From July 2025 to January 2026 I was a Corporate Customer Specialist Intern at MB Bank in Hanoi. The job was on the corporate side of the bank, so the customers were companies and not individuals.

I handled corporate account inquiries, pitched sales and helped carry operational feedback between internal teams. It was my first long look at how a bank runs day to day, in terms of who answers the client, who fixes the problem and how information moves between the two. That made it a good fit for someone studying economics who keeps drifting towards operations.
