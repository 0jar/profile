DAiTech is the AI and Technology Club at the Diplomatic Academy of Vietnam, and since May 2026 I have led its Applied Lab. I was asked to take the role on, which still surprises me a little, given that I am an economics student and not a computer scientist.

The lab's work is mostly applied operations. In practice that means getting data into a shape people can use, keeping the systems tidy, and building small web tools for the club. The people involved are fellow students who need things to work more than they need them to be impressive.

My rule for the tools is that they should be fast, plain and easy to hand over. A tool nobody else can maintain is a liability, so documentation is part of the job.
