I study international economics at the Diplomatic Academy of Vietnam in Hanoi, and I also spend a lot of my time cleaning up data, trackers, writing documentation, fixing websites. People keep telling me I look like an IT or communications guy, fair enough. My degree is in economics, but I think econ is only as good as the spreadsheets underneath it, so I fix those.

Languages are like that too. Translating terms for WordPress, Minecraft, Pronouns.page and writing in four languages feel almost the same as tidying a dataset, because somebody has to decide what a word means. I split my life between Hanoi and Tartu in Estonia. Depending on who you ask, I'm the quiet one at the back or the one laughing at his own dry joke. I occasionally write blog posts at [https://jarema.me](https://jarema.me) too.
