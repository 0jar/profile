---
question: "Are you a software engineer?"
order: 4
---

**No.** I study international economics, I write code to solve my problems. I can build tools, read other people's code and fix what I broke, but that's not really software engineering. Coding is just a way for me to work independently without waiting on someone else to fix a script or configure a server. I mostly care about whether a system reduces friction and lets me get back to whatever I was doing.
