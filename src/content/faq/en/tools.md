---
question: "Why do you keep choosing small, boring tools?"
order: 6
---

Because I'm the one who has to maintain them on a Tuesday night when something breaks. Boring tools have fewer ways to surprise me. Config files and standard Linux packages do one job predictably and the edge cases have probably already been discovered by someone else ten years ago. Interesting things are good for a hobby, but for anything I have to keep running, I want it dull.
