---
question: "How do you keep four languages going at once?"
order: 8
---

Vietnamese is my native language, but I'm more comfortable in English, I work and write in it by default. Estonian comes from the time spent in Slavic environments. I also know some Danish, Russian and Chinese, though absolutely nowhere near the level of the first three. I read local news, translate terms and switch between languages every day, and to be honest it works better for me than studying in the classroom. The part I find most fun is phonology, the tones, its lengths and its consonants.
